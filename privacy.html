This policy explains how SolidBinary ("we", "us") handles information collected through solidbinary.com. This is a general summary, not legal advice; please have it reviewed for your jurisdiction.

Information we collect

We collect the information you choose to send us, such as your name, business email, company, country, and project details when you submit an inquiry, email us, or book a call.

Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security and operation of the service.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and discuss potential projects.
  • To send you information you have asked for.
  • To keep records of our business communications.

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

Forms on this site may be processed by Formspree, and call bookings by a scheduling provider. These services process your data under their own privacy policies.

Retention

We keep inquiry information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws.

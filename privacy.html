This policy explains how SolidBinary ("we", "us") handles information collected through solidbinary.com.

Information we collect

We collect the information you choose to send us, such as your name, business email, company, country, and project details when you submit an inquiry or book a call.

Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security and operation of the service.

If website analytics are enabled, we collect aggregate usage data such as pages visited, referring sites and whether a form was submitted. We use it only to understand how the site is used and to improve it.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and discuss potential projects.
  • To send you information you have asked for.
  • To keep records of our business communications.

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

Inquiry form submissions are stored in Google Workspace (Google Sheets) and delivered to us by email through Google. Call bookings are handled by Google Calendar appointment scheduling, and calls take place on Google Meet. These services process your data under their own privacy policies.

Retention

We keep inquiry information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws.

Contact

For privacy questions, send us a message through our contact form.

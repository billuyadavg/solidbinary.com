## Answers in seconds, not a hunt through shared drives

Staff lose time looking for the latest policy, price list or process note, or asking the one colleague who knows. A knowledge assistant answers those questions from your own material.

## How it works

The assistant searches your documents for the passages relevant to a question, then writes an answer using only those passages. This approach is called retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). Because every answer is tied to a source, your team can check it with one click.

## Built to be trusted

- **Sources on every answer,** so nobody has to take it on faith.
- **"I don't know" when it doesn't know,** instead of guessing.
- **Permissions respected:** people only get answers from documents they are allowed to see.
- **Questions it couldn't answer are logged,** which shows you where your documentation has gaps.

## What we measure

Typically answer accuracy on a test set your team writes, share of questions answered, and time saved for the people who used to field them.

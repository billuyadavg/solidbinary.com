## The software around the AI

Agents rarely live alone. They need a dashboard for your team, an approval screen, a connection to an older system, or a product your customers log into. Product engineering covers that work.

## What we build

- **Internal tools and dashboards** for reviewing, approving and tracking what agents do.
- **Integrations and APIs** that connect agents to the systems that hold your data.
- **SaaS foundations:** sign-up, accounts, billing and multi-tenant architecture, for teams turning a workflow into a product.
- **Cloud setup:** deployment pipelines, monitoring, backups and access control.

## Modernising what you already have

If you rely on older systems, we improve them step by step: adding APIs, moving to the cloud and introducing AI features, without a risky full rewrite.

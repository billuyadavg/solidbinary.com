## Stop retyping the same data

Someone in most companies spends hours a week copying numbers from documents into a system, then chasing approvals. That work follows a pattern, and patterned work can be automated.

## What the agent does

1. **Collects documents** from an inbox, upload folder or portal.
2. **Extracts the fields you need:** supplier, amounts, dates, line items, terms.
3. **Checks them** against your rules and existing records, such as matching a purchase order or spotting a duplicate.
4. **Enters the data** into the right system, or sends it for approval.
5. **Puts anything uncertain in a review queue** for a person to confirm.

## Automation plus AI

Fixed rules handle the predictable steps. AI handles the parts that need reading and judgement. Every result carries a confidence check, and low-confidence items always go to a person.

## What we measure

Typically documents processed per hour, share handled without manual correction, and error rate compared with the current process.

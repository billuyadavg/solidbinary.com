## Agents need looking after

An agent that works on launch day can drift. Your tools change, your products change, and AI models are updated. Agent care keeps things working and keeps improving them.

## What's included

- **Monitoring:** errors, unusual activity and cost, with alerts when something needs attention.
- **Evaluation:** the agent is re-tested against your real examples on a regular schedule.
- **Updates:** prompts, models and integrations kept current, with changes tested before they go live.
- **Improvements:** small changes your team asks for, and new workflows when you're ready.
- **A monthly report:** what the agent handled, what it escalated, accuracy, and recommended next steps.

## How it's priced

A fixed monthly fee based on the number of agents and how much change you expect. You can pause or stop with notice.

## Every enquiry answered, every record up to date

Leads often wait hours, or days, for a first reply, while salespeople spend their afternoons updating the CRM. Both are patterned work an agent can take on.

## What the agent does

1. **Reads the enquiry** from your website form, inbox or chat.
2. **Qualifies it** against the criteria your team already uses, and flags anything it isn't sure about.
3. **Updates the CRM:** creates or finds the contact, tags it and assigns an owner.
4. **Drafts the reply** and waits for a person to approve it before anything is sent.

The same pattern works after meetings: the agent turns a call transcript into notes, next steps and CRM updates for a rep to check.

## What stays with your team

Pricing, commitments and anything sent to a customer are approved by a person. The agent works within limits you set, and every action is logged.

## What we measure

Agreed in the assessment, typically time to first reply, share of leads qualified without manual sorting, and hours saved on CRM updates.

## Background

Billu, who builds these agents, architected an AI revenue agent at Zime.ai that was used by more than 100 sales teams, including its calendar, call-recording and CRM integrations.

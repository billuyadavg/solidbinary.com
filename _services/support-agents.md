## Faster first replies without hiring for the queue

A large share of support tickets ask the same questions. An agent can sort them, answer the routine ones from your documentation, and hand the rest to your team with the context already gathered.

## What the agent does

1. **Reads each new ticket** and works out what it's about and how urgent it is.
2. **Finds the answer** in your help articles, policies or order data, and shows where it came from.
3. **Drafts a reply.** At first, a person approves every reply. Once accuracy is proven, you can let low-risk answers go out directly.
4. **Escalates** anything unclear, angry or sensitive, with a summary so your team doesn't start from scratch.

## What stays with your team

Refunds, complaints, account changes and anything outside the documentation. You decide where that line sits, and you can move it.

## What we measure

Typically first-response time, share of tickets resolved or drafted by the agent, and reply accuracy checked against a sample reviewed by your team.

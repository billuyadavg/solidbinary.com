## Engineering capacity, without the hiring delay

When your roadmap is bigger than your team, we provide engineers who join your workflow, tools, and rituals, and stay focused on your product.

## How it works

1. **Define the need:** skills, seniority, time zone overlap, and expected duration.
2. **Match the team:** we propose engineers whose experience fits your stack and goals.
3. **Onboard:** they join your communication channels, repositories, and planning.
4. **Deliver and adjust:** regular check-ins, and the flexibility to scale the team up or down.

## Flexible engagement

Teams can work on a monthly basis for continuous development, or alongside a fixed-scope project. See [Our Approach](/our-approach/#engagement) for engagement models.

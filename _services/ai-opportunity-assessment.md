## Find the one workflow worth automating first

Most companies have a long list of things AI could help with and no clear way to choose. The assessment gives you that choice, based on how your team actually works rather than on what's fashionable.

## What we look at

- **The work itself.** We sit with the people who handle leads, tickets, documents or internal questions, and map each step, tool and hand-off.
- **Your systems and data.** Which tools hold the information, how clean it is, and what an agent would need access to.
- **Volume and cost.** How often each task happens and roughly how much time it takes today.
- **Risk.** Where a mistake would be expensive, where personal data is involved, and where a person must stay in charge.

## What you receive

1. A ranked shortlist of AI and automation opportunities, scored on impact and effort.
2. A data and risk check for the top candidates.
3. A recommended first pilot: what it does, what it connects to, and the number it will be judged on.
4. A rough cost and timeline for that pilot.
5. A walkthrough call to go through the findings with your team.

Some opportunities won't need AI at all. If a rule, a form or a spreadsheet solves it, the report says so.

## How long it takes

Typically about two weeks, depending on how quickly we can speak with your team. It is a fixed price agreed before we start, so there are no surprises.

## After the assessment

You own the report and can act on it however you like. If you want us to build the first pilot, the assessment becomes its plan.

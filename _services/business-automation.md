## Give your team their time back

Copying data between tools, chasing approvals, and preparing the same reports every week take hours that could go to customers and growth. We map these processes and automate them reliably.

## What we automate

- Lead capture, routing, and follow-ups across your CRM, email, and WhatsApp.
- Invoice, order, and document handling with AI-powered data extraction.
- Employee onboarding, approvals, and internal requests.
- Scheduled reporting and dashboards from multiple data sources.

## Automation plus AI

Traditional automation follows fixed rules. Where a step needs judgement, such as reading an email, classifying a document, or drafting a response, we add AI with clear checks so exceptions go to a person.

## Start with the right problems

AI can help almost any business, but not every idea is worth building. We help you find the opportunities with clear value, realistic data requirements, and manageable risk.

## AI readiness assessment

A focused engagement where we:

- Review your key workflows, systems, and data.
- Identify and rank AI and automation opportunities by impact and effort.
- Flag risks around data quality, privacy, and security.
- Deliver a practical roadmap, including what to build first.

## From strategy to proof

Recommendations are only useful if they work in practice. Where it makes sense, we follow the assessment with a small proof of concept on your real data, so decisions are based on results rather than assumptions.

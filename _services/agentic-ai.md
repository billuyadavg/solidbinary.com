## AI that does the work, not just the talking

Most businesses have already tried a chatbot. Agentic AI goes further: agents that can read a request, look up the right information, use your business tools, and complete a task end to end, with a person approving the steps that matter.

We design agents around a specific job, give them only the access they need, and build in logging, review steps, and clear limits so you stay in control.

## Where agents help most

- **Customer operations:** triaging tickets, drafting replies, and resolving routine requests.
- **Sales and lead handling:** qualifying inbound leads, updating the CRM, and preparing follow-ups.
- **Back-office work:** reading documents, extracting data, and moving it between systems.
- **Internal knowledge:** answering staff questions from your own policies, documents, and data.

## How we build them

We start with one well-defined workflow, prove it works with real data, then expand. Every agent ships with evaluation tests, monitoring, and a human-in-the-loop path for exceptions.

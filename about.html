Who you'll work with

SolidBinary is a founder-led practice. When you hire us, you work with me, Billu Yadav, the person who scopes the work and writes the code.

I've spent 13 years building products in startups, most recently an AI revenue agent used by more than 100 sales teams. My background is below.

I started SolidBinary because most companies don't need an AI strategy deck. They need one workflow that works, measured honestly, and then the next one.

How we work

  • One workflow first. We prove value on a small pilot before anyone signs a bigger contract.
  • People approve what matters. Agents draft, sort and update; humans approve anything that's costly to get wrong.
  • Least access, full logs. Agents get only the permissions a task needs, and every action is recorded.
  • You own everything. Code, prompts, documentation and data are yours.
  • We tell you when not to use AI. If a rule, a form or a spreadsheet solves it, we'll say so.

A good fit if you

  • Have roughly 20 to 500 people
  • Can point to work that eats hours every week and follows a pattern
  • Keep that work in tools such as a CRM, helpdesk, inbox or shared drive
  • Can give a pilot an owner on your side who knows the process
  • Want to start small and decide on real results

Probably not a fit if you

  • Want a strategy report with no plan to build anything
  • Need a large team staffed from the first week
  • Want AI to make payment, legal or medical decisions without a person reviewing them
  • Are looking for the lowest hourly rate
